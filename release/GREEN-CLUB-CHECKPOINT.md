# Dark-green club and playable football — code20

The owner rejected the light cream/ticket direction and requested the earlier green, then clarified that playing matches should resemble the direct control of older mobile football games. This build replaces the active light CSS and decorative football stickers with forest-green surfaces and the familiar lime #c1f66b. Club home combines identity, actual stadium progression, fixture, both match choices and metrics. Auth, career, navigation, contact/legal pages and marketing share the same green palette. Original slogans, private support address/contact form, existing clubs and the January1 2027 at10CET public gates are preserved. No GooglePlay release.

Stadium geometry is rebuilt. Both the lightweight clubhouse SVG and the playable Three.js scene use stadium-layout.js: stepped seating outside the field, roofs supported over the seats, proper goal nets and a plinth containing every structure. The SVG auto-fits every camera angle, with explicit ground/marking/structure painter layers. Static SVG was inspected through Inkscape rasterization; outputs/stadium-redesign.png is an illustration, never a gameplay screenshot. The website uses the matching, explicitly labelled illustration with a new cache key.

New clients explicitly request manualVersion2 for league, domestic cup, Europe and personal career. The server snapshots eleven-a-side formation, ratings and stadium stage. shared/football11.js runs 11v11 on a60x96 field with deterministic movement, AI, passing, shooting, sprint, actual physical goals and keeper saves. The original engine is retained unchanged as arcade-v1.js; older installed clients continue to receive version1 challenges and finish existing games identically. Results remain server-replayed, timing-bound and paid once. No arbitrary client scores are accepted.

Manual matches smooth player/ball poses between simulation updates and have an articulated low-poly team, instanced crowd, three cameras, screen-relative broadcast controls, a controlled-player marker, radar and possession indicator. The player's career remains locked to their own position. Simple graphics uses its original screen axes. Portrait layout keeps pitch and touch controls together; landscape remains available. Games are explicitly short60-second, simplified football, without offside or throw-ins. This is not a claim of FIFA-level graphics or literal4D.
