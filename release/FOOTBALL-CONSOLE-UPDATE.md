## Implemented

Clubhouse is rebuilt as an image-led football game menu: a large actual next-fixture card with original player artwork, existing simulation and manual controls, live match resume, true club metrics, separate player-career and Europe tiles, a real domestic-cup destination, three working squad/transfer/academy shortcuts, stadium controls and live economy. Cup navigation explicitly opens the cup workspace. No invented online modes or decorative unimplemented game buttons were added. Wide/landscape and portrait menus have distinct layouts.

Manual play now uses a full-viewport pitch with a compact scoreboard, close player-follow camera by default, circular A/B/C actions over the field and left-hand joystick. A shoots/tackles/dives; B passes/switches/calls; hold C to sprint. Keyboard equivalents remain Space/E/Shift and WASD. Camera heading snaps on a view change so screen-relative input stays aligned. TV/whole-field cameras, quality selection and simple fallback remain. The close camera was checked with real Three.js projection math at both field edges in portrait and landscape. Portrait radar is placed beside the toolbar, away from the controlled player and action buttons. Help explicitly says the match clock continues; this is not a new pause protocol.

Three.js players have animated elbows/knees, face details, kit numbers, selection indicator and contact shadows. The grass uses a small deterministic procedural texture, and the pitch plane is explicitly above the walkway slab so depth testing cannot hide it. Ground-layer separation is covered by geometry checks. Broadcast camera is closer and lower. Pixel ratio/FPS caps and GPU resource disposal remain. Camera preferences migrate once to the new close camera and retain subsequent explicit choices. Gameplay physics, rewards and legacy replay remain unchanged: matches are simplified60-second11v11 games with no offside/throw-ins/free-kick system, not a claim of licensed-game visual fidelity or new full football rules.

The website uses the same slate identity and an original player-led showcase, explicitly labelled an illustration. Its countdown and January1 2027 at10CET public APK/web gates remain. Original slogans, private support email/contact form and saved accounts/clubs are preserved. No GooglePlay release.
