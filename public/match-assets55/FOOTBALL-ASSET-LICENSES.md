# Touchline footballer source and redistribution notices

The exported GLB was generated locally on 2026-11-13. It is an original assembly of MakeHuman CC0 anatomy and photographic skin/eye/eyebrow assets, a generated buzz cut, and custom blue football shirt, white shorts, blue socks and slim dark shoes. It depicts a fictional adult man, not a licensed footballer or team. It contains no EA/FIFA, Dream League Soccer, or third-party game extraction and no Mixamo assets or animations.

## Animations

The supplied final GLB has no embedded animation. The game uses separately baked locomotion tracks plus original football action poses.

Ara also includes CMU motion-capture-based clips. The game includes baked Walk, Run, Strafe and original Idle/Cheer tracks. The CMU motion data may be used in commercial products, but may not be resold on its own. Acknowledgement: The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.
